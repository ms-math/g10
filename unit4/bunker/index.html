Diefenbunker Lockdown

You're a new signals clerk at the government's secret emergency headquarters, four storeys under a farm field west of Ottawa. During a surprise drill, the master control jams. The blast doors seal and the air plant drops to reserve.

Climb from the Bank of Canada vault on Level 100 to the blast tunnel. Search each room for measurements, solve its terminal, and collect the six code digits that open the blast doors.

Between floors you'll stop for a reading checkpoint: a short document with multiple-choice questions, or a 3-minute maze. The air clock pauses while you read.

The terminals get tougher as you climb. In rooms 1 and 2 the readings go onto the diagram for you. In rooms 3 and 4 you place them yourself. In rooms 5 and 6 you pick the right sketch first, and one reading needs a second look.

The story is made up. The bunker and its rooms are real: it's now Canada's Cold War Museum.
