Where in Ottawa?

On Friday night, a thief known as the Magpie stole a 400-year-old astrolabe from the Canadian Museum of History. You have one weekend to follow the trail across the city and make the arrest.

  • 1At every stop a witness gives you a lead. Solve it to find the Magpie’s next place on the city grid.
  • 2In a scored case, wrong answers, hints and wrong trips cost hours on the case clock. The Magpie’s train leaves on Sunday.
  • 3Twice you’ll stop to read, like the reading part of the OSSLT. The clock stops while you read, and good reading wins time back.
  • 4At the last stop, use similar triangles to find the Magpie’s height, then pick the thief out of a lineup.

The story is made up. The places and the history on every postcard are real. Bring a calculator and some scrap paper.
