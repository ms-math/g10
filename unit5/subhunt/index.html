SUB HUNT

Battleship by substitution

An enemy submarine fleet is hiding somewhere in a 10 by 10 grid. Every depth charge you drop is aimed by a linear system, and you have to solve it to fire.

  • Each turn you have three firing solutions. Each one lands on a different square, and you only know where once you solve it. Fire the one that lands where you want.
  • A depth charge hits a 3 by 3 area around the point you fire at.
  • A wrong answer is a misfire: you lose your shot and the enemy gets a free torpedo.
  • Sink all three enemy subs before they sink your fleet.

Bring scrap paper. Every battle has a new fleet and new systems.
